# AIA Qi-informed mockup guidance

Design UI mockups in the spirit of AIA Qi: consistency, reuse, efficiency, scalability. This is **not** a source of official Qi design tokens or copyrighted component implementations. Tokens in tokens.json are draft suggestions requiring AIA design-owner approval.

- Trace every screen, field and workflow to an approved Business Requirement; never invent approval outcomes or integrations.
- Use semantic tokens (primary, surface, text, border, success, warning, danger); do not claim exact official shades.
- Provide forms, error/empty/loading/success states, accessible labels, focus visibility and mobile layout.
- Use compact clear navigation, consistent controls, minimum 44px touch targets and readable contrast, subject to verification.
- Generate structured screen specifications first. Treat previews as drafts, not functioning services.
- Do not include production data, confidential records, proprietary logos or unlicensed artwork in mockups.
- Prefer supplied AIA-authorized Figma/Qi assets when available; they supersede these examples.

## Component inventory

Use `kit/ai/component-catalog.json` from the same pinned main-branch catalog snapshot for components, classes and supported states. Do not invent official Qi API or corporate brand compliance. The catalogue is a local *independent, unofficial* adaptation and requires design-owner review before production.
